DCM sizing logic
  • Target Power: Nth-percentile of daily peak load values.
  • Target Energy: Target power × on-peak window length.
  • Viable when on-peak demand charge ≥ $8/kW.

TOU Arbitrage sizing logic
  • Target Energy: Nth-percentile of daily on-peak consumption.
  • Target Power: Target energy ÷ on-peak window length.
  • Viable when on-peak minus off-peak energy spread ≥ $0.08/kWh.
